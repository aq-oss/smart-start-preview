How it works

Your month, told plainly.

Four beats, in order. Nothing is asked twice, and nothing happens to your file that you cannot see.

(01)The free assessment

One sitting. Four answers.

Questions on income, obligations, debt, savings and what you are working toward. One per screen. The result appears on screen before you create anything.

Five bands. Your reading sits in one of them.

  • 01A readiness score, and the band it falls in.
  • 02The single largest obstacle, named in plain language.
  • 03A verdict on whether your goal is reachable as things stand.
  • 04A next step you can take straight away, or leave.

The readiness score is Smart Start’s own read. It is not a credit score.

(02)The goal, and the date

An amount, a date, and a picture of it.

You name what you are working toward and when you want it. We record your baseline, work out the monthly surplus behind it, and say whether that date holds.

The honest part

If your date does not work on your surplus as it stands, we say so, and show you the one that does.

(03)Documents, and a plain reading

Three documents and your report.

You upload them once. We never pull your report on your behalf, and we never ask for the same thing twice.

(04)The work, stage by stage

Nine stages, and you can see all of them.

Every change is notified. Stages that run for weeks carry an expected by date. We publish no duration for any stage, because the bureaus and the creditors set that clock, not us.

The learning track runs underneath, on its own clock, from the day you join.

(05)Who does the work

Three parties, and only three.

You

You name the goal, upload the three documents and your report, and upload what arrives in the post while the work is running.

Smart Start

We assess your readiness, read your report back to you plainly, hold the goal and the date, and show you every change as it happens.

An attorney affiliated partner

A partner organisation sets the strategy, files disputes, contacts creditors, evaluates what comes back and recommends what happens next.

Requires confirmation The partner’s scope across the credit bureaus is stated here once it is confirmed.

(06)What we never promise

Four things you will never hear from us.

  • No guaranteed outcome.
  • No promised change to your score.
  • No promised removal of any item.
  • No legal advice.
