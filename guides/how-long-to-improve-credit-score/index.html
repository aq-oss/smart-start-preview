Guide

How long it really takes to improve a credit score.

Some of it can move inside a month. Some of it only time changes. Knowing which is which is the difference between a plan and a wish.

In short

  • Utilisation and corrected errors are the fastest things to move.
  • Age of history, and the footprint of a serious event, move only with time.
  • Disputes run on a statutory clock set by law, not by any service.

(01)Two speeds

What moves, and what does not.

The mechanism matters more than the calendar. A credit file changes when somebody reports something new to a bureau, and not before.

WhatWhy it moves at that speed
Card utilisationYour lender reports a balance on its own cycle. A lower balance is visible the next time it reports.
An error, once correctedThe bureau updates the file when the reinvestigation concludes.
A recent hard inquiryIt loses weight within months and falls off the file within two years.
Length of credit historyOnly time changes it. Nothing else does.
A missed paymentIt ages and loses weight gradually, but it stays on the file for years.
A paid collectionPaying it settles the debt. The record of it does not vanish because it was paid.

(02)The statutory clock

A dispute has a legal window, not a promised one.

When you dispute something on your credit report, the Fair Credit Reporting Act requires the credit bureau to reinvestigate within a statutory window, set out at 15 U.S.C. 1681i. That window belongs to the law. It is not a service level that anybody, including us, can shorten by trying harder.

That is why Smart Start never publishes a duration for any stage of the work. What we do publish is where your file has got to, and an expected by date on a stage that we know runs for weeks.

(03)The myths

What is being sold, and what is behind it.

None of this is stupidity on the buyer’s part. These claims are engineered to be believable.

01

100 points overnight

Sold because it is what people want to hear. Behind it is usually one utilisation change, on one card, in one month, presented as a method.

02

A 700 in 30 days

A number and a deadline together sound like a plan. Neither the bureaus nor the creditors work to anybody else’s deadline.

03

We can remove anything

Accurate, properly reported information does not come off because somebody asked firmly. What comes off is what is wrong.

(04)Two clocks

One clock is not yours. The other one is.

This is how Smart Start handles the waiting: the file moves at the speed the bureaus and creditors set, and the learning track moves at yours.

The file, on the bureaus’ clock

Moves when a creditor reports or a reinvestigation concludes. Nobody can speed this up, and anybody claiming to is selling you something.

The learning track, on yours

Short lessons, points, a weekly streak. It runs from the day you join and does not wait for anybody else.
