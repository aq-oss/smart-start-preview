The address on my utility bill

You · 5 March

I moved in January so my utility bill still has my old address on it. What should I send instead?

Smart Start · 5 March

A bank statement or an insurance letter dated in the last three months works just as well, as long as it shows the address on your license. Upload it in place of the bill and we will check it.
