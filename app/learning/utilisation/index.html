Why a balance you clear every month still counts

Back to the track

Lesson

Your lender reports a balance to the credit bureau once a month, on its own cycle. The figure it reports is whatever was owing on the day the statement was produced.

So if you spend most of your limit and clear it in full a week later, the bureau still sees the high balance. You paid perfectly, and the file shows somebody running close to their limit.

The fix is timing rather than discipline. Paying down before the statement date, rather than after it, changes what gets reported. Nothing else about your behaviour has to change.
